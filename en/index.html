Concepts

Concept

Categorical latents

1 source

A latent representation made of several categorical variables, each taking one of a fixed set of classes, instead of continuous Gaussian variables.

 Concept

KL balancing

1 source

A way of training latent dynamics models in which the KL term between posterior and prior updates the prior faster than it updates the posterior.

 Concept

Latent overshooting

2 sources

A training objective for latent sequence models that, besides one-step predictions, also trains multi-step predictions by pulling them toward the states inferred from real observations, entirely in…

 Concept

Latent space planning

2 sources

Choosing actions by searching over candidate action sequences inside a learned latent dynamics model and scoring them by predicted rewards, without generating observations.

 Concept

Learning in imagination

3 sources

Training an agent's policy on experience generated by a learned world model instead of by the real environment.

 Concept

Mixture density RNN

1 source

A recurrent neural network whose output layer gives the parameters of a mixture of Gaussians, so it predicts a probability distribution over the next value instead of a single value.

 Concept

Model-based reinforcement learning

4 sources

Reinforcement learning that learns or uses a model of how the environment responds to actions, and uses that model to decide how to act or to learn how to act.

 Concept

Recurrent state-space model

3 sources

A latent dynamics model whose state has a deterministic part, carried forward by a recurrent network, and a stochastic part sampled at every step.

 Concept

Variational autoencoder

4 sources

A neural network that encodes an input into a small random latent vector and learns to decode that vector back into the input.

 Concept

World model

4 sources

A learned model of an environment that predicts what will happen next, usually given the current state and an action.
